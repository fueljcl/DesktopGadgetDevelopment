# DesktopGadget 1.0.0 — Data handling

Published October 31, 2026. This describes the application, official website and enquiries. Free Marketplace registration and language-pack distribution are available.

## Information stored on your PC

The application stores settings, gadget positions, notes, agenda entries, selected sources and destinations, permissions, history and related information on your PC. Credentials and protected stored information use protection tied to your Windows account. Not every settings file is encrypted. Manage access to your backups and exported files as well.

Uninstalling the application does not automatically remove all settings and user data. To remove settings, keep any necessary backup, close the application and inspect the documented storage location before deleting its contents. Files saved elsewhere and information held by external services are managed separately.

## External connections

Application update checks retrieve official update information. Checking at startup is optional and off by default. The check does not attach your diagnostic report or the text of notes and agenda entries. The destination processes connection information such as the IP address needed for communication.

When you enable external information sources or services such as AI, requests and content selected for that function are sent to its configured destination. Some enabled functions fetch information periodically. Review the destination, content and costs. Services opened in a browser also operate under that service's and browser's practices.

The free Marketplace handles an ID, authentication public keys, recovery verification information, sessions and records needed for submitting and obtaining works. These are used for authentication, entitlement checks, delivery, operation and abuse prevention. The Marketplace does not synchronise all of your local notes and agenda entries. Account deletion is available through the application. Deleting an account is distinct from handling published works, operational records and backups.

## Diagnostics and enquiries

A development support report is displayed for you to review and can then be saved locally. It is not uploaded automatically. The current report includes version, feature counts and read/render failure counts; it is designed to exclude note and agenda content, device identity, paths, URLs, recordings and credentials. Review any separate attachments or screenshots before sending them.

For email or Discord enquiries, sender contact information, the enquiry and attachments are used to respond, investigate and maintain a support history. They are retained for the necessary period and information no longer needed is cleared. Do not attach passwords, private keys or identity documents to bug reports. Other participants may be able to see information posted in a public Discord channel.

## Website, protection and deletion

The official site and delivery infrastructure use Cloudflare, the email contact uses Google, and the community uses Discord. Those services also process information associated with access and enquiries. We have not added custom advertising-tracking or analytics scripts to the public website pages.

Measures include access controls, signature verification, Windows protection facilities and avoiding unnecessary secrets in logs. Backup copies do not necessarily disappear at the same time as the original data. Contact us below about access, correction or deletion. We check your relationship to the information to the extent needed and respond according to applicable law and what is actually retained.
